# Help

## What is Stern?

Stern keeps your notes, documents, passwords and other secrets safe on your computer. You use it like a chat: type what you want, and Stern does it. Everything you save is locked with your Stern password, and nothing is kept online. There is no account to create and nothing to sign up for.

## What should I keep in Stern?

Stern is made for your notes and documents: things you want kept private and out of the cloud, such as a shopping list, a will, a recipe, or a letter you do not want anyone else to read. You can also save passwords, cards, and contacts, but that is not what Stern is mainly for.

## How do I save a note?

Tap "Save a note", or type "save a note". A box opens in the chat with a place for the title and a larger place for your text. Write the note, then tap Save note. If you change your mind, tap Cancel and nothing is saved. Your note is shown just as you typed it, so you can read it back and fix mistakes before you save.

## How do I change a note I saved?

Type "edit" followed by the note's name, for example "edit my shopping list". The same box opens with the note's title and text already in it. Change what you like, then tap Save note. If two notes have similar names, Stern lists them and asks which one you mean. Type a little more of the name to pick one. Only one note can be open at a time: save it or cancel it before you open another, so what you typed is never lost by accident.

## How do I save a document?

Tap "Save a document", or type "save a document". Stern asks what the document is called, then asks for its text. Paste the text in, and Stern shows it back to you so you can check it before you confirm. A document is pasted text. You cannot attach a file yet.

## How do I save a password?

Tap "Save a password", or type something like "save my GitHub password". Stern asks a few short questions, one at a time: the website, the username, and then the password. When it asks for the password itself, type it in the box. Each letter shows for a moment and then turns into a star, in case someone is looking over your shoulder. Stern never writes a password back into the chat.

## How do I find something I saved?

Type something like "find my shopping list" or "show my GitHub password". If more than one thing matches, Stern shows them as buttons so you can tap the one you mean. If you ask for something that is not there, Stern tells you it could not find it.

## What is the safest way to keep something forever?

Keep it in a plain text file. A text file ends in .txt, and every computer can open it with no special app, now or years from now. Nobody can shut it down, change its price, or take it away. Store the file on your own computer, and keep a copy on a USB stick. Stern is a good place for things you want locked. A plain text file is best for things you must never lose.

## What is my Stern password?

It is the one password you create when you first open Stern. It protects everything you save, and you enter it each time you open Stern. It is not stored anywhere that Stern or anyone else can read it back to you.

## What if I forget my password?

Nobody can get your information back, not even the people who made Stern. That is how your information stays private. Write your password down and keep it somewhere safe. If it is lost, the only way forward is to start over, which erases everything saved in Stern.

## Can I change how Stern looks?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and pick Light or Dark under Look. The change happens straight away. You can do this before you sign in too. Stern remembers your choice the next time you open it. Light is what you get the first time.

## What is an AI model?

It is a small program that understands what you type. When you say "save a note", the AI model works out what you mean, and Stern does the rest. It runs on your computer, so what you write never leaves it. Stern needs one AI model before the chat will work.

## Which AI model should I choose?

Small is quick and a small download. It handles short, simple requests well, but it can misread longer or unusual wording. Large understands more ways of saying things and makes fewer mistakes, but it takes a few seconds to reply, is several times bigger to download, and uses more of your computer's memory. Not sure? Choose Small. You can switch to the other one at any time in Settings, once you have signed in. Before you sign in, Settings shows your models but cannot change them.

## How do I remove an AI model?

Open Settings, find the model under AI model, and tap Remove. Stern asks you to confirm first, because removing a model deletes it from your computer to free up space. Your notes and passwords are not touched. If you remove the model you are using, Stern will not understand your messages until you choose another one. You can download any model again whenever you like. You need to be signed in to remove a model.

## Why does Stern download something?

Stern does not come with an AI model, which keeps the app small. When you choose an AI model, Stern downloads what it needs and keeps it on your computer. This is the only thing Stern uses the internet for. Your passwords and notes are never sent anywhere. When you remove your last AI model, Stern also removes what it downloaded to run it.

## The download did not work. What now?

Check that you are online, then try again. Some work or school networks block downloads. If it keeps failing, try a different network. A download that stops part way does not harm anything you already have, so it is safe to try again.

## What does Lock do?

Lock hides everything until you enter your password again. Stern also locks when you close it. The padlock at the top right is open when Stern is unlocked and closed when it is locked. Tap it to lock Stern. Stern asks "Lock Stern?" first, so you do not lock it by accident. If you would rather it did not ask, turn off Ask before locking under Locking in Settings. You can only change this after you sign in. When Stern is locked, tapping the padlock takes you to the place where you type your password.

## Why can't I unlock right now?

After 5 wrong passwords, Stern makes you wait 5 minutes before you can try again. This stops anyone from guessing your password. Wait for the time to pass, then type your password carefully and try again.

## Is my information safe?

Everything you save is scrambled (encrypted) and stays on your computer. How safe it is also depends on you: use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and keep your computer secure. Stern cannot protect a computer that someone else controls. Please read the disclaimer for the full terms.
