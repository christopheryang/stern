# Help

## What is Stern?

Stern keeps your passwords and other secrets safe on your computer. You use it like a chat: say what you want, and Stern does it. Nothing is kept online.

## What should I keep in Stern?

Stern is made for your notes and documents: things you want kept private and out of the cloud. You can also save passwords, cards, and contacts, but that is not what Stern is mainly for.

## How do I save a note or document?

Tap "Save a note" or "Save a document", or type "save a note". Stern asks for a name, then for the text, and shows it in full so you can check it. To write a longer note or change one later, open Notes in the top bar. There you can see your notes, and create or edit them in a text box.

## How do I save a password?

Tap "Save a password", or type something like "save my GitHub password". Stern asks a few short questions, one at a time. When it asks for the password itself, type it in the box.

## How do I find something I saved?

Type something like "find my shopping list" or "show my GitHub password".

## What is the safest way to keep something forever?

Keep it in a plain text file. A text file ends in .txt, and every computer can open it with no special app, now or years from now. Nobody can shut it down, change its price, or take it away. Store the file on your own computer, and keep a copy on a USB stick. Stern is a good place for things you want locked. A plain text file is best for things you must never lose.

## What is my Stern password?

It is the one password you create when you first open Stern. It protects everything you save, and you enter it each time you open Stern.

## What if I forget my password?

Nobody can get your information back, not even the people who made Stern. That is how your information stays private. Write your password down and keep it somewhere safe. If it is lost, the only way forward is to start over, which erases everything saved in Stern.

## Can I change how Stern looks?

Yes. Tap the sun or moon at the top right, on any screen, even before you sign in. You can also pick Light or Dark under Look in Settings. Stern remembers your choice. Light is what you get the first time.

## What is an AI model?

It is a small program that understands what you type. When you say "save a note", the AI model works out what you mean, and Stern does the rest. It runs on your computer, so what you write never leaves it.

## Which AI model should I choose?

Small is quick and a small download. It handles short, simple requests well, but it can misread longer or unusual wording. Large understands more ways of saying things and makes fewer mistakes, but it takes a few seconds to reply, is several times bigger to download, and uses more of your computer's memory. Not sure? Choose Small. You can switch or remove a model at any time in Settings.

## Why does Stern download something?

Stern does not come with an AI model, which keeps the app small. When you choose an AI model, Stern downloads what it needs and keeps it on your computer. This is the only thing Stern uses the internet for. Your passwords are never sent anywhere.

## The download did not work. What now?

Check that you are online, then try again. Some work or school networks block downloads. If it keeps failing, try a different network.

## What does Lock do?

Lock hides everything until you enter your password again. Stern also locks when you close it.

## Why can't I unlock right now?

After 5 wrong passwords, Stern makes you wait 5 minutes before you can try again.

## Is my information safe?

Everything you save is scrambled (encrypted) and stays on your computer. How safe it is also depends on you: use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and keep your computer secure. Please read the disclaimer for the full terms.
