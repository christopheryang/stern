# ADR-NNNN: Title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, the alternatives
that were genuinely on the table. State facts, not preferences.

## Decision

What was decided, in terms concrete enough to act on.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision — the benefits that motivated it, the
costs it imposes, and the follow-up work or risks it creates. Include
the negative consequences; an ADR that lists none is a sales pitch, not
a record.
